# Benchmark results

AgentIndex on 7 real knowledge bases, with 215 questions taken from real tasks, each asked as a natural question and as search keywords. Generated by `python3 -m bench run` on 2026-11-04 with agentindex 0.2.1, Python 3.13.18 and SQLite 3.53.1 (Linux x86_64). How it works: [knowledge/development/benchmarks.md](../knowledge/development/benchmarks.md).

## Context cost

Approximate tokens (characters / 4) of text a simulated agent reads. Loading everything is paid in every session; AgentIndex costs a 174-token CLAUDE.md pointer per session plus each lookup. Lookups are medians over natural questions, and misses count all their reads. The grep agent here reads whole files, which is an upper bound: real agents read the matching parts, and [real-agents.md](real-agents.md) has what they spent.

| Corpus | Load everything | grep, reading whole files | AgentIndex, per lookup | Lookups before AgentIndex costs more |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rust compiler dev guide | 477,233 (does not fit) | 11,401 | **2,859** | 166 |
| Node.js contributor docs | 142,756 | 15,246 | **2,253** | 63 |
| Electron docs | 509,347 (does not fit) | 43,796 | **2,613** | 194 |
| GitLab development docs | 2,088,152 (does not fit) | 38,087 | **3,865** | 540 |
| PostHog AGENTS.md | 11,758 | 3,288 | **1,699** | 6 |
| Next.js AGENTS.md | 7,868 | **1,208** | 1,745 | 4 |
| workerd AGENTS.md | 6,702 | **1,548** | 1,641 | 3 |

"Does not fit": more than a 200k-token context window.

## Corpora

Each corpus is pinned to a commit and indexed as it is, with no per-corpus tuning. Questions were written from real merged PRs (or common tasks) without looking at the docs; the answers were labeled separately, and questions the docs do not answer were dropped. Two labelers worked independently on every question, and a third reconciled every disagreement. The last two columns are how often the two labelers agreed to keep or drop a question, and how often their labels (docs and sections) were identical. In every question both labelers answered, they named at least one answering doc in common.
